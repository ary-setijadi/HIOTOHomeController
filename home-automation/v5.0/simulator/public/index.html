Registering issues a client certificate (CN = serial) and a QR code. Scan it with the registration app (node server.js in v5.0/registration, then open http://127.0.0.1:3007) to onboard the device.

Create device

A new device connects over MQTTS with its own certificate. Add a cert for its serial in v5.0/certs/ to let it authenticate.
